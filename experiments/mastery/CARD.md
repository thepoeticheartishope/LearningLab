# Experiment card — Mastery tiers

Written **before** the build. Verdict filled in after the play-test.

- **Claim:** climbing tiers (bet + pick → evidence tap → type → rest) gets a
  fact to "I can type it cold days later" better than plain multiple-choice
  drilling.
- **Test:** pick ~30 CompTIA A+ facts I don't know cold on the Pick screen.
  Practise them over several sessions across about a week. The Lab logs every
  answer (tier, bet, right/wrong, time) so the log can back up the feel.
- **Keep if:** Timothy's call by feel after the play-test.
- **Kill if:** Timothy's call by feel after the play-test.
- **Verdict + date:** keep / tweak / kill — YYYY-MM-DD
- **Notes:**
  - Tiers: 1 Practicing (pick from 4), 2 Nearly mastered (tap the evidence;
    only the 8 facts with a scenario have it, the rest skip 1 → 3),
    3 Mastered check (type it), 4 Mastered (comes back as a typed check).
  - 5 credits on a tier move a fact up; a miss drops it one tier.
  - Rests after a move up: tier 2 = 3 questions + 1 day, tier 3 = 6 + 3,
    Mastered = 15 + 7 (longer after each kept check).
