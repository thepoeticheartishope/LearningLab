# Experiment card — Bubble answers

Written **before** the build. Verdict filled in after the play-test.

- **Claim:** answering by tapping floating bubbles feels better (more fun,
  more "want one more round") than tapping plain buttons, without hurting
  accuracy.
- **Test:** CompTIA port facts (24 questions, both directions), rounds of 8.
  Modes alternate each round (the page suggests the next mode). About one
  week, his play-test. Log: mode, right/wrong, first try, ms to answer.
- **Keep if:** he prefers bubbles and first-try accuracy is about the same
  (within ~10 points) as buttons.
- **Kill if:** buttons feel the same or better, or bubbles feel fiddly / slow.
